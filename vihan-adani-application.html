The Work That's Most Relevant to This Role
Most Directly Relevant
791,000 enrolments. 7 months. Treating enterprise like consumer.
At Deloitte Digital, I was embedded with a Fortune 500 life sciences client to build and scale a donor loyalty and rewards programme — starting from zero, scaling to 220+ centers across the US. The brief was essentially identical to what Adani OneApp needs to do: take a physical consumer touchpoint that's traditionally transactional, add a digital rewards layer, and change user behaviour through it.

What made it work: I launched MVP at 10 centers first. Validated the core mechanic (do rewards actually change donation frequency?) before building the ecosystem. When the A/B data showed a 5% lift in donation frequency within a 12,000-donor test cohort, I used that signal to accelerate the full rollout.

What I learned that's directly applicable to Adani: the most important decision in a loyalty programme isn't the reward structure — it's defining exactly what behaviour you're trying to change and then measuring it with precision.
791,000+ enrolments · 50%+ adoption rate · 220+ locations · 7 months
The Experiment I Killed
200,000 users. 8 weeks. Called it when the data was clear.
We ran a gamified onboarding journey for 200,000 new donors — step completion mechanics, loyalty points on first action, visible progress indicators. The hypothesis: engagement with the journey would create momentum that translated to the core behaviour (donation).

The data showed statistically insignificant lift versus control. Users engaged with the game. They just didn't donate more frequently. I recommended killing the feature and redirecting investment to loyalty tier mechanics.

Why this story matters for Adani: Adani Rewards is launching into exactly this risk — that users collect points without the programme meaningfully changing their behaviour at the airport. The design discipline to measure the right metric (behaviour change, not engagement) and the courage to act on what the data shows — that's what I'd bring to the OneApp product team.
Experiment correctly killed · Resources redirected · Higher-performing mechanic shipped
